Welcome to E-challan Portal! Check your traffic fines online. Follow traffic rules and drive safely!

How to Pay Your E-Challan

  1. Visit the official E-challan portal.
  2. Enter your vehicle registration number.
  3. Check Your Pending Traffic Challans.
  4. Select the challan you want to pay.
  5. Complete the payment process.
  6. Download your payment receipt.

Important Terms

E-Challan
An electronic notice issued for a traffic violation.
Vehicle Registration Number
A unique number assigned to a registered vehicle.
Challan Number
A reference number used to identify a particular challan.
Payment Receipt
A document confirming that a payment has been completed.

About E-Challan

The E-Challan Management System helps citizens view and manage traffic violation notices electronically.

Citizens can check challan details, review violation information, and proceed to the appropriate payment service.

Why Use the E-Challan Portal

The online system provides convenient access to challan information and reduces the need to visit a traffic police office for routine enquiries.
